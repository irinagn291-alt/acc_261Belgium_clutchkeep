Version 1.0 is the first release of Clutchkeep.

• Onboarding pages: The roost is the yard book, Tap a hen, write a credit, and Pullet, then Layer.
• Flock roost of named hens. Add a hen. Tap a hen to log an egg.
• Pullet then Layer: the first Credit writes a LayMark.
• Production, Mash and Dose on the Flock pager.
• Analytics for Yield, Cost versus yield, Health, Eggs / Layer, Cost per egg, FCR and 7-day predict.
• Settings for Export CSV, Contact Clutchkeep, Haptics, Re-run onboarding and Reset all data.
